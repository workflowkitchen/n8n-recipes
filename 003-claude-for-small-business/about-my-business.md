## What we sell
Maple Street Bakery, Austin TX. Sourdough, pastries, coffee, custom cakes. Retail shop (Tue–Sun, 7am–3pm, closed Mondays), wholesale to cafes and delis, catering for events.

## Who our customers are
- Walk-in regulars (Square).
- Wholesale accounts: weekly orders, invoiced in QuickBooks, net 15.
- Catering: offices and event planners, invoiced net 7, 50% deposit for orders over $500.

## Prices
- Sourdough loaf $9 retail / $6 wholesale. Croissant $4.50 / $3.
- Catering: pastry tray (24 pcs) $85, breakfast box per person $14, delivery in Austin $35.
- Custom cakes from $65. Rush orders under 48 hours +20%.

## How we talk to customers
Warm, short, first names. No exclamation marks in money emails. Sign as "Anna, Maple Street Bakery".

## Who approves money
Anna (owner) approves every payment, refund, discount and any email about money. Nothing gets sent or paid without her OK.

## Jobs we repeat every week
- Monday: check cash, last week's sales, who owes us.
- Tuesday: chase overdue invoices (remind at 15 days late, call at 30).
- Wednesday: answer catering inquiries with a priced proposal.
- Thursday: plan next week's social posts from what sold best.
- Friday: payroll is every other Friday; end of month, match sales to the bank for the accountant.
